Biography

Sundar Pichai is the Chief Executive Officer of Google and Alphabet Inc. Born in India, he studied at IIT Kharagpur and later earned degrees from Stanford University and the Wharton School. Pichai joined Google in 2004 and played a key role in the development of products like Google Chrome, Chrome OS, and Android. His leadership and vision helped Google expand its global reach and innovate in multiple technological fields. Known for his calm leadership and strategic thinking, Sundar Pichai is widely respected as one of the most influential leaders in the tech industry today.
